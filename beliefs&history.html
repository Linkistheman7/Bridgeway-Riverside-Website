Meet Our Pastor

Pastor Joe Ackermann began serving in ministry at the age of 13 on Easter morning, when he volunteered to help with his church’s bus ministry simply because he wanted to lend a hand. What began as a simple act of service grew into a lifelong calling. Over the years, God led Pastor Joe through many areas of ministry, including teaching Sunday School, serving as a bus ministry director and youth director, and ultimately leading him to pastoral ministry. Since October 2016, Pastor Joe has faithfully served as the pastor of Bridgeway Riverside Church, which was planted that same year. His heart is to lead the church to love God and love others, while continually growing in affection for and knowledge of our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ. Pastor Joe and his wife, Lisa, were married on October 22, 2005. Even before they were married, they shared a passion for serving the Lord together in many areas of ministry. Today, they are blessed with three children—Lylah, Joel, and Jace—who also faithfully serve alongside their family with a desire to make Christ known to others. Pastor Joe is grateful for the opportunity to serve the Bridgeway Riverside family and is passionate about seeing people grow in their relationship with Christ, serve others, and live out the Gospel together.
