Who We Are

Rooted in Community,
Open to Everyone

Bridgeway Riverside is a community-first church nestled in the small town of Moscow, Ohio. We believe faith comes alive when people come together — around a table, on a trail, or in a pew — and we work hard to be a place where everyone genuinely feels they belong.

Whether you've been walking with God for decades or you're just starting to ask the big questions, we think there's a seat here for you. Come as you are, and see what happens.

— Pastor & the Bridgeway Family
